## Overview

The **Highlighter Bluetooth Speaker** is a portable audio device project that reimagines the familiar form of a stationery highlighter as a compact consumer electronics product.

The project combines Bluetooth audio playback, rechargeable power, miniature speaker integration, and product-focused hardware design within a distinctive, stationery-inspired form factor.

Developed through hands-on prototyping and component integration, the project explores the intersection of embedded electronics, industrial design, portability, and practical consumer product development.

## Project Highlights

- **Distinctive Product Design** — An unconventional alternative to conventional portable speaker form factors.
- **Portable Audio** — Bluetooth-enabled audio playback in a compact product concept.
- **Embedded Hardware** — Practical integration of electronic components and rechargeable power.
- **Compact Product Engineering** — Focus on portability, component integration, and enclosure utilization.
- **Hands-on Prototyping** — Experience in hardware selection, assembly, integration, and product refinement.
- **Future Development** — Planned improvements to audio functionality, hardware integration, and manufacturability.
